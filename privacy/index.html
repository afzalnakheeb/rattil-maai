Privacy

Privacy policy

Recite With Me is built for Quran students, their teachers and their families. We collect only what the app needs to work, we show no advertising, and we never sell data. Last updated 2026-10-30.

What we store

  • Your Google account basics when you sign in: name, email address and profile photo, used to identify you and to show you to your linked teacher or students.
  • Your learning record: your hifz plan, daily progress, adhkar counts, app settings, recitation recordings, the marks on each recording, grades and teacher comments.
  • Class links: which teacher a student has joined with a class code.
  • Teacher applications: if you apply to teach, the details you give (languages, experience, ijazah, bio, availability and an optional WhatsApp number). Approved teachers' profile cards are shown to signed-in users; the phone number is seen only by academy admins.
  • Teacher requests: if you ask for a teacher, the student's name, languages, age group and notes, seen by academy admins and the teacher you are matched with.

Who can see it

Your recordings and marks are kept in private storage. Only you and a teacher you have joined can open them. A teacher can see the plan progress of students who joined their class. You can leave a teacher’s class at any time in Settings.

Video sessions

Video calls connect the teacher’s and the student’s devices directly and are encrypted in transit. The connection is set up through our server, which does not receive the video. The teacher’s device records the session audio, which is saved with the marks and grade.

Speech recognition

The AI recitation check uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition. Depending on your browser, the audio may be processed by the browser maker’s service (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari) under their own privacy terms.

Prayer location

The city or coordinates you choose for prayer times and the Qibla stay on your device. They are not sent to our servers.

Where data is kept

The app is hosted on Vercel. Accounts, records and recordings are stored with Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt).

Your choices

You can delete your own recordings in the app, sign out at any time, and ask us to delete your account and all of its data. Children should use the app with a parent’s or teacher’s permission.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact details for the Recite With Me team will be listed on this page.
