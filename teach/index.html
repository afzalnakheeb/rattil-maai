For teachers

Teach the Quran with Recite With Me

We welcome qualified Quran teachers from every country. Students and parents ask the academy for a teacher in their language — Arabic, English, Urdu, Malay, Indonesian, Turkish, French, Bengali and more — and we match them with a verified teacher who speaks it.

Apply to teachAbout the app

How it works

  • Apply with your Google account: your languages, years of teaching, ijazah and sanad, studies, a short bio and the times you are free.
  • We review every application. Approved teachers get a verified profile that students and parents can see.
  • Students are matched to you by language, by teacher gender if the family asks, and by the number of students you want.
  • Teach live on free video in the browser. The AI listens on the student's phone and shows you what it hears; you confirm, add your own marks and grade out of 100.
  • Every session is recorded with your marks, and parents get weekly and monthly reports.

Who can apply

Teachers of Quran reading (nazra), Noorani Qaida, hifz, tajweed and ijazah, for children and adults. Male teachers and female teachers are both needed, especially for girls and women.
