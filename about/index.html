About

Memorize the Quran with a daily plan, an AI that listens, and your teacher

Recite With Me — رتّل معي, “Rattil Maʿī” — is a free web app for memorizing the Quran to the standard of Masjid al-Haram and Masjid an-Nabawi: Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim, Madinah Mushaf. It joins the three things a student needs in one place: a clear daily plan, an AI that listens every day, and a teacher who checks and grades.

For students

  • A daily hifz plan with the new lesson (sabaq), recent revision (sabqi) and old revision (manzil). Choose half a page, one page or two pages a day.
  • Listen first in a page-by-page Madinah Mushaf with 34 reciters, including the imams of al-Haram and an-Nabawi, ayah highlighting, repeat and speed control.
  • Recite from memory while the words stay hidden. The AI follows you word by word, marks skipped, wrong and extra words straight away, and plays the correct word or ayah.
  • Every recitation is recorded with its marks, so you can hear the exact moment of each mistake.

For teachers

  • A class code your students join with their Google account.
  • Free live video sessions in the browser. The AI listens on the student’s phone and you see its suggestions live; confirm them, dismiss them or add your own marks.
  • Grade out of 100 (hifz 40, tajweed 30, makharij 20, waqf 10) with a comment and a pass or repeat decision. The whole session is recorded.
  • Review and grade students’ AI practice recordings, and hold in-person sessions on your own device.

For parents

Weekly and monthly reports show active days, time spent reciting, new pages, teacher grades and comments, the number and type of mistakes, and the exact words to revise. Share them on WhatsApp or save them as a PDF.

Daily worship

Morning, evening, after-prayer and sleep adhkar with a counter; short and full Ruqyah; a Hisn al-Muslim duas library; prayer times by your country’s method (Umm al-Qura, UAE Awqaf, Muslim World League and more) with a Qibla compass. Quranic texts always come from the Mushaf text, never retyped.

Sources

Quran text: Tanzil Uthmani (Madinah Mushaf, Hafs) via Al Quran Cloud. Recitations: Islamic Network CDN and EveryAyah.com, ayah by ayah. Adhkar and duas: Hisn al-Muslim, with references. Fonts: Alexandria, IBM Plex Sans and Amiri Quran.
